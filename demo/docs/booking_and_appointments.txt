FADE MASTERS BARBERSHOP — BOOKING & APPOINTMENTS
================================================

HOW TO BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
---------------------------
We offer multiple ways to book your slot at Fade Masters:

1. WhatsApp — Send us a message on WhatsApp at +254 712 345 678. Tell us your preferred date, time, and which barber you'd like. We'll confirm your slot within 15 minutes during business hours.

2. Phone Call — Call us directly at +254 712 345 678. Our front desk is available during opening hours.

3. Walk-In — We welcome walk-ins any time we are open. However, appointments always take priority. During busy periods (weekends and evenings), walk-in wait times can be 30–60 minutes.

4. Instagram DM — Message us on Instagram @FadeMastersNBO and we'll book you in.


APPOINTMENT SLOTS
-----------------
Appointments are available in 30-minute and 60-minute slots depending on the service:

- Shape Up / Line Up: 30 minutes
- Classic Haircut / Taper: 45 minutes
- Skin Fade / Mid / High / Low Fade: 45–60 minutes
- Kids Cut: 45 minutes
- Cut + Beard Combo: 60 minutes
- Beard Services only: 30 minutes
- Hair Treatments (dreadlocks, cornrows): 60–120 minutes

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, especially for weekends.


SAME-DAY BOOKINGS
-----------------
Same-day appointments are available subject to barber availability. WhatsApp or call us in the morning to check availability. We do our best to accommodate same-day requests.


BOOKING CONFIRMATION
--------------------
After booking, you will receive a WhatsApp confirmation message with:
- Date and time of your appointment
- Name of your assigned barber
- Service booked
- Reminder sent 2 hours before your appointment


CHOOSING YOUR BARBER
--------------------
You are welcome to request a specific barber when booking. If your preferred barber is not available at your chosen time, we will suggest the next available slot with them, or offer an alternative barber.

See our "Barbers & Team" document for barber profiles and specialties.


CANCELLATIONS & RESCHEDULING
------------------------------
We understand plans change. Here is our policy:

- Cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before your appointment: No charge, full refund if prepaid.
- Cancel less than 2 hours before: A KES 200 cancellation fee applies.
- No-show (did not arrive, no notice): A KES 300 no-show fee applies and will be charged on your next visit.

To cancel or reschedule, WhatsApp or call us as early as possible.


GROUP BOOKINGS
--------------
Booking for a group of 3 or more (e.g. groomsmen packages, team grooming)? Contact us at least 48 hours in advance so we can arrange enough barbers and a private time slot.

Group discounts are available for 4+ people. Ask us for details.


WAITING TIMES (WALK-INS)
--------------------------
Estimated walk-in wait times:
- Weekday mornings (8am–12pm): 0–15 minutes
- Weekday afternoons (12pm–5pm): 15–30 minutes
- Weekday evenings (5pm–8pm): 30–45 minutes
- Saturdays: 30–60 minutes
- Sundays: 20–40 minutes

You can WhatsApp us to check current wait time before coming in.


PREPAYMENT
----------
Appointments do not require prepayment. However, for group bookings of 4 or more, a deposit of KES 500 per person is required to secure the booking.

Deposits are fully refundable if cancelled 24 hours in advance.
