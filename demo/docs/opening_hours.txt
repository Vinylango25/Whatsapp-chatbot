PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
---------------
We remain open on most public holidays with slightly reduced hours:

Public Holiday Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

We are CLOSED on the following days:
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Good Friday (date varies)
- Easter Sunday (date varies)

On all other public holidays (Jamhuri Day, Madaraka Day, Mashujaa Day, Labour Day, etc.)
we are open with holiday hours (9am–5pm).

We will always post any changes to our WhatsApp status and Instagram stories at least
2 days in advance.


PEAK HOURS (BUSIEST TIMES)
---------------------------
These are our busiest times. Walk-in wait can be 45–60 minutes. Book ahead if possible:

- Friday evenings: 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM
- Saturday: All day, especially 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
- Last week of the month (paydays)
- Before public holidays


QUIETEST TIMES (SHORTEST WAIT)
--------------------------------
If you prefer a quick walk-in with little waiting:

- Monday to Wednesday mornings: 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons: 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM


AFTER-HOURS APPOINTMENTS
--------------------------
We occasionally accommodate after-hours appointments for special occasions
(weddings, events, photoshoots). These carry a KES 500 after-hours surcharge.
Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange.
