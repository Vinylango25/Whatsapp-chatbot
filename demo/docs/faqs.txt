FADE MASTERS BARBERSHOP — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
======================================================

Q1: Where are you located?
---------------------------
We are at Westlands Square, Ground Floor, Shop 4, Westlands, Nairobi.
Next to the Equity Bank branch. Free parking is available on-site.
WhatsApp us at +254 753 592 868 for directions.


Q2: What are your opening hours?
----------------------------------
Monday to Thursday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Friday: 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Saturday: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Sunday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

We are closed on Christmas Day, New Year's Day, Good Friday, and Easter Sunday.


Q3: How do I book an appointment?
-----------------------------------
WhatsApp us at +254 753 592 868 with your preferred date, time, and service.
We confirm within 15 minutes. Walk-ins are also welcome, but appointments
get priority — especially on weekends.


Q4: How much does a haircut cost?
-----------------------------------
Prices start from KES 300 for a shape up.
Classic Haircut: KES 500
Skin Fade / Bald Fade: KES 700
Kids Cut (under 12): KES 400
Cut + Beard Combo: KES 900

See our full Services & Pricing document for the complete menu.


Q5: Do you do kids cuts?
--------------------------
Yes! We love cutting kids' hair. Our barbers are patient and experienced
with children. Kids cuts (under 12) are KES 400. We recommend booking
to avoid long waits, as kids can get restless.


Q6: Do you accept walk-ins?
-----------------------------
Yes, we welcome walk-ins any time we are open. However, booked appointments
take priority. Walk-in wait times on weekends can be 30–60 minutes.
WhatsApp us to check current wait time before coming.


Q7: Can I request a specific barber?
--------------------------------------
Absolutely. When booking, mention the barber you'd like. We'll confirm
their availability. Our barbers: Brian (head barber), Kevin, Sharon, James, and Tony.
See our Barbers & Team document for their specialties.


Q8: How do I pay?
------------------
We accept:
- Cash (KES)
- M-Pesa (Paybill or Till — ask at front desk for details)
- M-Pesa via WhatsApp (our AI can help you pay before you arrive)

We do not currently accept card payments.


Q9: Do you have a loyalty program?
------------------------------------
Yes! Fade Rewards is free to join. Earn points on every visit and redeem
them for free cuts and discounts. WhatsApp us "Join Loyalty Program" to sign up.
Referring a friend earns you 25 bonus points.


Q10: Do you do beard services only (no haircut)?
--------------------------------------------------
Yes, you can book beard-only services. A beard trim is KES 300, hot towel
shave is KES 500, and beard design / lining is KES 350.


Q11: How long does a typical appointment take?
-----------------------------------------------
Shape Up / Line Up: 20–30 minutes
Classic Haircut: 40–45 minutes
Skin Fade: 45–60 minutes
Cut + Beard Combo: 60 minutes
Kids Cut: 40–45 minutes
Dreadlock Maintenance: 60–120 minutes (depending on length)


Q12: What if I'm running late for my appointment?
---------------------------------------------------
Let us know via WhatsApp as early as possible. We hold your slot for up to
15 minutes past your appointment time. After 15 minutes, your slot may be
given to a walk-in. A KES 200 late fee applies if you arrive 15–30 minutes
late and we have accommodated you.


Q13: Do you offer services for women?
---------------------------------------
Yes! Sharon, our barber and stylist, specialises in female clients. She offers
ladies fades, cornrows, creative designs, and eyebrow shaping.
Several of our treatments and beard services are also popular with female clients.


Q14: Do you sell hair products?
---------------------------------
Yes, we stock a range of professional hair and beard products in-shop including
beard oils, edge control, scalp treatments, shampoos, and conditioners.
See our Products For Sale document for the full list and prices.


Q15: How do I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
----------------------------------------------------
WhatsApp or call us as early as possible. Cancellations at least 2 hours before
your appointment are free. Cancellations under 2 hours incur a KES 200 fee.
No-shows incur a KES 300 fee charged on your next visit.
