FADE MASTERS BARBERSHOP — HAIR CARE & AFTERCARE TIPS
======================================================

Getting a great cut is only half the job — maintaining it is the other half.
Here are our expert tips to keep your hair and beard looking fresh longer.


AFTER YOUR HAIRCUT
------------------

First 24 Hours:
- Avoid washing your hair for at least 24 hours after a fresh cut. This lets
  the natural oils settle and keeps the cut looking sharp.
- Avoid sweating heavily (gym, long runs) on the same day if possible.
- If you use styling products, apply lightly — less is more right after a fresh cut.

Day 2 Onwards:
- Wash your hair 2–3 times per week maximum. Over-washing strips natural oils
  and makes the scalp dry and flaky.
- Always use a good conditioner after shampooing — especially if you have
  natural or curly hair.
- Pat dry with a towel — do not rub vigorously as this causes frizz and
  can irritate the scalp.


FADE MAINTENANCE
----------------
A good fade starts to grow out after 2–3 weeks. To keep it looking sharp:

- Come in for a shape up or line up (KES 300) every 2 weeks between full cuts.
- Moisturise your scalp with a light oil (jojoba or coconut oil) to avoid
  dryness and flaking, especially along the faded areas.
- Avoid picking or scratching the scalp — this can cause irritation and bumps.


BEARD CARE
----------
Daily Beard Routine:
1. Wash your beard 2–3 times a week with a dedicated beard wash (not regular soap).
2. Apply beard oil daily — 2–4 drops massaged into the beard and skin underneath.
3. Comb or brush your beard in the direction of growth every morning.
4. Use a light beard balm to style and hold throughout the day.

Weekly:
- Trim stray hairs with scissors or a trimmer to maintain shape between visits.
- A full beard trim at the shop every 3–4 weeks keeps everything in check.

Common Beard Problems:
- Beard itch: Usually happens in the early growth phase. Use beard oil daily —
  it soothes the skin and reduces itch.
- Dandruff (beardruff): Exfoliate the skin under your beard gently and use a
  moisturising beard wash.
- Patchiness: Some patchiness is genetic. Keep the beard hydrated and avoid
  over-trimming patches — let them fill in naturally over time.


SCALP HEALTH
-------------
- A healthy scalp = healthy hair growth. Massage your scalp with fingertips
  (not nails) for 2–3 minutes daily to stimulate blood flow.
- Eat a balanced diet rich in protein, iron, and vitamins A, C, D, and E —
  these directly support hair growth.
- Stay hydrated. Dehydration shows in your hair before your skin.
- If you have persistent dandruff, itching, or thinning, see a dermatologist.
  Our barbers can recommend when a professional opinion is needed.


DREADLOCK CARE
--------------
- Retwist every 4–6 weeks to keep new growth tight and neat.
- Wash locs every 1–2 weeks with a residue-free shampoo.
- Oil your scalp between washes — not the locs themselves, which can cause buildup.
- Sleep on a satin pillowcase or wrap locs in a satin scarf to reduce frizz and breakage.
- Avoid heavy wax products — they cause buildup that is very hard to remove.


CORNROW CARE
------------
- Keep cornrows moisturised with a light oil spray daily.
- Sleep with a satin bonnet or scarf to protect edges and reduce frizz.
- Do not leave cornrows in for more than 4–6 weeks to avoid tension damage to the hairline.
- When taking out cornrows, do it gently — detangle from the ends upward.
