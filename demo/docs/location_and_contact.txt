FADE MASTERS BARBERSHOP — LOCATION & CONTACT
=============================================

ADDRESS
-------
Fade Masters Barbershop
Westlands Square, Ground Floor, Shop 4
Westlands, Nairobi
Kenya

We are on the ground floor of Westlands Square, next to the Equity Bank branch.
Look for our blue and black signage at the entrance.


HOW TO FIND US
--------------
From Westlands Roundabout:
- Head towards Sarit Centre
- Turn left at the first junction after the roundabout
- Westlands Square is 200 metres on your right
- We are on the ground floor, Shop 4

From CBD (City Centre):
- Take Waiyaki Way heading towards Westlands
- Pass the ABC Place junction
- Westlands Square is on your left just before the Westlands roundabout

Landmark: Directly opposite the Total petrol station on Waiyaki Way.


PARKING
-------
Free parking is available in the Westlands Square parking lot.
Enter from the main gate off Waiyaki Way.
Parking is available for up to 2 hours free of charge with a parking ticket
stamped at our front desk.
Additional parking is available at the nearby Sarit Centre (paid).


PUBLIC TRANSPORT
----------------
Matatu: Routes 23, 44, and 106 stop at the Westlands stage, a 3-minute walk.
Boda Boda: Boda boda riders are available at the Westlands roundabout stage.
Uber / Bolt: Search "Westlands Square, Nairobi" — drop-off directly at the gate.
