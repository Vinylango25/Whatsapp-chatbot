HOW TO REQUEST A SPECIFIC BARBER
----------------------------------
When booking via WhatsApp, simply mention the barber's name:
  "I'd like to book a skin fade with Brian on Saturday at 10am"

We will confirm availability and let you know if your preferred barber
is not available at that time.

Please note: Specific barber requests are subject to their availability.
Walk-in customers are served by whoever is next available.


BARBER TIPS
-----------
Tipping is not mandatory but is always appreciated. Our team works hard
and a tip of KES 50–200 is a great way to show appreciation for
excellent service. You can tip via cash or M-Pesa.
