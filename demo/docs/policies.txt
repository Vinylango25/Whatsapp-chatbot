FADE MASTERS BARBERSHOP — POLICIES
====================================

We aim to give every client the best experience. These policies help us run
smoothly and ensure fairness for all clients and our team.


APPOINTMENT & CANCELLATION POLICY
-----------------------------------
Booking Confirmation:
Your appointment is confirmed once you receive a WhatsApp confirmation message
from us. Please ensure your details (date, time, service, barber) are correct.

Cancellation — 2+ hours notice:
No charge. If you prepaid a deposit, it is fully refunded within 24 hours.

Cancellation — Less than 2 hours notice:
A KES 200 cancellation fee applies. This covers the barber's reserved time.

No-Show (no notice, did not arrive):
A KES 300 no-show fee applies. This will be charged on your next visit before
service begins. Repeated no-shows may result in pre-payment being required
for future bookings.

Late Arrivals:
- Up to 15 minutes late: We will do our best to accommodate you.
- 15–30 minutes late: A KES 200 late fee applies. Service may be shortened.
- More than 30 minutes late: Your appointment may be forfeited and treated
  as a no-show.

Please WhatsApp us if you are running late — we appreciate the communication
and will always try to work with you.


REFUND POLICY
--------------
We take pride in our work and want every client to leave happy.

Satisfaction Guarantee:
If you are not satisfied with your service, please let us know before you leave
the shop. We will make adjustments at no extra charge.

After Leaving the Shop:
If you raise a concern within 24 hours of your visit, we will invite you back
for a complimentary correction.

Refunds:
Cash refunds are not offered for completed services. However, we will always
correct any issue free of charge. If we are unable to correct the issue to
your satisfaction, a credit toward a future service will be offered.

Prepaid Deposits:
Deposits for group bookings are refundable if cancelled 24 hours in advance.
Deposits are non-refundable for same-day cancellations or no-shows.


CHILDREN'S POLICY
------------------
Children under 12 are welcome and served at a discounted rate of KES 400.

- A parent or guardian must be present for all children under 12.
- For safety, children must remain seated and calm during the cut.
- If a child is too distressed to continue, the service will be paused.
  We will not force a child through a cut — safety first.
- Barbers reserve the right to stop a service if the child's movement
  poses a safety risk (sharp tools).

We recommend:
- Booking a morning slot when children are freshest and the shop is quieter.
- Bringing a distraction (phone video, snack) for young children.
- Our barber Kevin is especially patient with children and is a great choice
  for first-time young clients.


HYGIENE & HEALTH POLICY
------------------------
Your safety and health is our priority:

- All tools (clippers, razors, scissors) are sterilised between every client
  using hospital-grade disinfectant.
- Single-use razor blades are disposed of after each use — never reused.
- Fresh clean towels and cape for every client.
- Barbers wash hands before every service.

Please inform your barber of any scalp conditions, skin sensitivities,
or allergies before your service. We use gentle products but it is important
we know in advance.

If you have an active scalp infection, open wounds on the scalp, or contagious
skin conditions, we kindly ask you to reschedule until fully healed. This
protects both you and other clients.


CONDUCT POLICY
--------------
We welcome everyone and maintain a respectful, inclusive environment.

- Abusive, threatening, or discriminatory behaviour toward staff or other
  clients will not be tolerated. We reserve the right to refuse service.
- No smoking or vaping on the premises.
- Loud phone calls: We ask that you step outside for calls during your service.
- We are a family-friendly environment — please be mindful of language.


PRICING POLICY
--------------
- All prices displayed are final and inclusive of VAT.
- Prices are subject to change. We will always notify clients of price changes
  via WhatsApp and Instagram at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Promotional prices and discounts cannot be combined unless explicitly stated.
- Student and Senior discounts require valid ID at time of service.


PRIVACY POLICY
--------------
We collect your WhatsApp number and name for booking and loyalty program purposes only.
We do not share your contact details with third parties.
To opt out of WhatsApp communications, reply STOP at any time.
